Privacy Policy for Earbit

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

Earbit does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

Earbit keeps your learning data on your phone only. It is never sent anywhere:

  • Learning progress: which words and grammar you have heard, when each is next due for review, and which ones you tapped as not understood.
  • Lesson history and statistics: how long you listened each day and how many words you know.
  • Your place in the story: which episodes you have heard.
  • Settings: your level, lesson length, story amount, playback speed, and similar preferences.

This data lives in the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling Earbit removes all of it. Like any app's data, it may be included in your device backups if you have iCloud or computer backups turned on; those backups are handled by Apple and your own settings.

Speech

The Japanese audio is recorded into the app and plays from your phone. English is spoken by your device's built-in speech synthesizer, on the device. Earbit sends no text or audio to any service.

Exporting your progress

Settings has an option to export your progress as a file. The file goes only where you choose to send it with the system share sheet. Earbit never receives a copy.

Network access

Earbit does not use the internet. All stories, sentences, audio, and fonts are bundled in the app.

Children

Earbit does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
