Kikitori

Learn Japanese by listening: a story told at your level, one new word at a time.

Common questions

The audio stops when I lock the phone.

Check that the ring/silent switch and Low Power Mode are not muting it, then open Kikitori and press Play again. Lessons are meant to keep playing with the screen locked.

A lesson is too easy or too hard.

Settings → Starting level lets you run Find my level again or pick a level. Settings → Start over erases what you have learned and lets you choose again.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app?

No. Progress is kept on your phone across updates. It is removed only if you delete the app. Settings → Advanced → Export progress saves it as a file.
